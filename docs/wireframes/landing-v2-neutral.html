How the card gets made

First, paste the posting.

Greenhouse and Workday postings fill in the company, role and location. Anything else you type in yourself, and nothing saves until you've looked it over.

New application Autofilled from Greenhouse

Check the fields, then save. Nothing is saved automatically.

CancelSave application

Then let the silence count.

Choose how long quiet means ghosted. Past that, an application moves to Ghosted on its own, so a dead lead stops looking alive. Interviews never auto-ghost; that call stays yours.

Then read where it stalls.

A rejection before any interview and a Failed Interview/OA after one are different problems with different fixes. The funnel keeps them apart, and anything still waiting stays open instead of counting as a loss.

Your board stays yours

Nothing on your board is public. Companies, roles, notes and every status stay with you. A recap carries only the totals and rates for the range you pick, and it leaves JourneyJob only when you download it or share it.
